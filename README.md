## CTupe - Watch & Download YouTube Videos on muOS  

**CTupe** is a lightweight YouTube player for **muOS**, allowing you to watch and download videos smoothly with an optimized experience for your ambernic rg35xx devices.  

### 🚀 Features  
- Watch YouTube videos directly on **muOS**  
- Download videos for offline viewing   
- Optimized performance for low-power devices  
- Supports video playback controls
